PocketFox Companion Privacy Policy

Your data stays on your phone.

Effective September 2026 · Applies to PocketFox Companion on iOS and the web

PocketFox Companion is a personal ERP (Exposure and Response Prevention) tracker for OCD. It does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any of your information. Everything you enter is stored only in your device's local storage — there is no account, no login, and no server this app talks to.

What the app stores

Everything you choose to enter stays on your own device, saved to your browser or app's local on-device database (IndexedDB):

  • Session logs — exposure hierarchy, SUDS ratings, notes
  • Journal entries — Morning Anchor, Wind Down, Quick Prompts, Intrusive Thought and Obsessive Thought worksheets
  • Focus Plan entries and debriefs
  • Fear Ladders, Values, and Flare Guide content you create
  • Optional photos you attach to a session

What this app does not do

  • No account or sign-in, anywhere in the app
  • No analytics, tracking pixels, or third-party SDKs
  • No server — nothing you enter is ever transmitted off your device
  • No selling or sharing of information, because none is ever collected off-device to begin with

Your data, your control

  • Export a full backup as a JSON file you keep, any time, from Settings
  • Permanently delete all local data from the app, any time, from Settings
  • Uninstalling the app or clearing browser/site storage also permanently erases everything stored locally

Children's privacy

PocketFox Companion does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children — because it does not collect information from anyone at all.

Changes to this policy

If a future version ever adds a feature involving data leaving your device, this page will be updated first, and the change will be described in that version's release notes.
